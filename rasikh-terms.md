## 2. Prayer times, alerts and the qibla

Prayer times are calculated on your phone from your location and the method you choose. The qibla direction
comes from your phone's compass, which can be thrown off by metal, magnets and other devices nearby. Alerts
depend on your phone's settings and battery rules, which we do not control.

We work hard to get these right, but they are estimates. Where it matters, check them against your local
mosque, and do not rely on an alert alone for a prayer you cannot afford to miss.
