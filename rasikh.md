Rasikh is a daily-practice app for Muslims, made by TJB Ventures FZE. It gives you accurate prayer times for
where you are, adhan notifications, the qibla direction, a private record of your prayers and practice, and
short lessons and duas.

Your location and your record of practice stay on your phone. An account holds your email address and little
else. You can sign in with your email address, with Apple or with Google.
