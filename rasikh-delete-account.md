Rasikh is made by TJB Ventures FZE. This page is for anyone with a Rasikh account, on Android or iPhone.

## In the app

Open Rasikh, then **Settings**, then **Account**, then **Delete account**, and confirm. Your login, your account
record and any feedback you sent are deleted at once, with no waiting period. The app also asks PostHog to
delete the usage events attached to your account and AppsFlyer to delete what it holds for your device.

## Without the app

Write to **rasikh@tjbventures.ai** from the email address of your Rasikh account, with the subject
"Delete my account". If you write from another address we will ask you to confirm from the one on file, so
that nobody else can delete your account. We delete it within one month and tell you when it is done.

## What is deleted, and what is not

- **Deleted:** your login, your account record (email address, account identifier, creation date, name if
  Google or Apple supplied one, the date a one-time offer was shown), your feedback, and the usage and
  attribution data held for your account.
- **On your phone, not with us:** your record of practice, your location and your settings never reach us.
  Remove them with the Delete control on the Tracker's data screen, or by removing the app.
- **Your purchase record at RevenueCat** is not removed by deleting the account, because it is needed to
  honour a subscription. Ask us in the same email and we will have RevenueCat delete it. A subscription
  itself is cancelled in Google Play (or, for one bought on iPhone, through Apple); deleting the account does
  not cancel it.
- **Kept by law or by the store:** Google and Apple keep their own record of a purchase under their privacy
  policies.
