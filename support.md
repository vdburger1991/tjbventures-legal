## Common questions

## How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. On your iPhone, open
**Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Orla** to change your plan, turn
off auto-renewal, or cancel. Cancelling stops future charges; your access
continues until the end of the period you've paid for.

## I subscribed but the app isn't unlocked

On the subscription screen, tap **Restore Purchases**. If that doesn't
resolve it, make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID that made the
purchase, then email us.

## How do I delete my data?

In the app: **Settings → Delete all my data**. This permanently removes
everything the app holds. Deleting is not reversible — and we can't restore
your records for you, because we never had them: your data lives on your
device, not on our servers.

## How does Orla use Apple Health?

Only if you give permission, and read-only: Orla displays wrist temperature,
heart rate variability, sleep, and heart rate alongside what you've logged.
It never writes anything to Apple Health, and never stores or transmits
Health data. You can change permissions any time in iOS **Settings →
Privacy &amp; Security → Health → Orla**.

## Where can I read about privacy?

The full policy is at [legal.tjbventures.ai/privacy.html](https://legal.tjbventures.ai/privacy.html),
and the plain-language disclaimer at
[legal.tjbventures.ai/disclaimer.html](https://legal.tjbventures.ai/disclaimer.html).
The short version: no ads, no data selling, no third-party analytics, and
your health entries stay on your device.

## Something is broken

Email us with your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when
it happened. Screenshots help enormously. We take bugs seriously —
especially anything touching your records or your export.
